## Transcription downloads

- Fix a download that sat at 0% for around nine minutes before anything happened. The step that measures progress was allowed to retry for that long before the actual download was permitted to start, and it reported nothing while it did — so a transfer that was fine looked wedged, then finished on its own with no explanation
- Stop and resume a transcription download. There was previously no button at all: no pause, and no download either, because the progress row replaced it. Stopping keeps every file that already arrived
- Show progress when it is known, instead of requiring byte counts that the fallback never provides

## AI models

- Transcription and Intelligence are now one page, **AI Models**, with sections named after what each model does: **Voice to Text** for writing down what was said, and **Text summarisation & more** for summaries and chat
- One-click setup sits at the top and fetches only what is missing
- It no longer replaces a model you already chose. If you transcribe through a cloud provider, or already summarise with a model that is not the recommended one, that half is left alone
- Downloading a Qwen transcription model turns on English, Chinese, Spanish, German and French, added to any languages you already had

## Elsewhere

- Recordings moved from AI to General in settings, since it is about captured audio rather than models
- The transcription models are called the same thing everywhere. Logs and messages still said "Soniqo Qwen3 1.7B" where the settings said "Qwen3 ASR 1.7B"
