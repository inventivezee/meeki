## Fixes

- During a batch, every recording was being restarted every five seconds instead of being left to finish. The progress count stayed frozen while transcription carried on, and most of the work done was thrown away and begun again. Each recording now runs once, and the count keeps step with it
- The language model was also being loaded twice for most recordings: once by the check that runs every few seconds, then again a moment later when the summary needed a larger working window. Both now agree on the size up front, so it loads once
