## Summaries

- Choose how much detail a summary keeps: **Brief**, **Balanced** or **Detailed**, under **AI Models** in settings. Summaries already grew with the length of the meeting — this sets how generous that is, so a long meeting still produces more than a short one whichever you pick
- Balanced is the default and matches how summaries were written before, so nothing changes unless you change it
- Detailed asks for the supporting detail, numbers and specifics that were previously compressed away, and gives each properly discussed topic its own section

## Recording controls

- **Resume** shows a play triangle instead of the red dot. The dot means "start capturing" everywhere else in the app, and Resume adds to a recording that already exists
- **New Recording** shows a red square in place of the plus
- The record button in the sidebar sits clear of the buttons beside it, rather than flush against them
