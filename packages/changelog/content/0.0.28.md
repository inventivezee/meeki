---

## Fixes

- A recording with no speech in it was treated as a failure rather than a finished one, so it was never marked done and came back at the front of the queue every time the import was started — failing again in a fraction of a second, indefinitely. Where many imported files are silent this is most of why a large import never seemed to shrink. Silence now counts as a result, and those recordings leave the queue for good
