## Fixes

- Transcribing imported recordings could take the whole app down. Transcription and the language model both run on the graphics processor, which will only reserve about three quarters of your Mac's memory, and on a 16 GB machine the language model's share left too little for transcription. The request failed, and the failure was not one the app could recover from — it simply quit. The language model is now released before each recording and loaded again for its summary
- Clicking **Start Recording** twice started two recordings. Only one of them could show a stop button, so the other kept running with no way to end it. A second click now opens the recording already starting instead
