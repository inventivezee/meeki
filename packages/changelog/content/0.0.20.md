## Fixes

- A recording with no speech in it was treated as a failed save. It warned that the transcript could not be saved and that the recording had been kept so you could try again — then tried again on the next launch, and every launch after that, reaching the same empty result each time
- Nothing was ever wrong with the save; there was simply nothing to transcribe. Meeki now says so once, as a note rather than an error, and leaves the recording alone. The audio stays on the note, and **Re-transcribe** is still there if you think the detection was wrong
