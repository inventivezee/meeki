## Fixes

- The app could close itself part-way through transcribing. Recordings are transcribed in 25-second pieces, and whatever is left at the end of the file becomes a final short piece — sometimes only a few milliseconds. That was too short for the model to read, and it stopped the app outright rather than reporting a problem. Any recording whose length happened to leave a small remainder would do it, which is why it seemed to strike at random. Pieces too short to hold anything are now skipped
- A recording was being moved on from as soon as its transcript was written, while its summary was still being generated — so the summary was cut off seconds after it started, leaving a note with nothing in it. One import finished 143 transcripts and only 19 summaries this way. Each recording is now held until it has genuinely finished, and the progress count keeps step with it
- Updating while an import is running no longer reports a crash on restart. Transcription is now brought to a stop before the app is replaced

## Improvements

- Settings → Recordings can now write only the missing summaries, without waiting behind every recording still to be transcribed
