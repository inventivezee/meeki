## Chat

- Fix the AI chat refusing to open. Clicking the green sphere replaced the panel with **Something went wrong!** and `isValidProp is not a function`, so there was no way in
- The cause was an animation library shipped without one of its optional pieces. Two copies of it ended up in the app, and the second reused a failed lookup from the first and treated the empty result as usable, which broke every animated element it drew. The missing piece is now included, so nothing has to guess
